## Why can't I sleep?

Insomnia usually starts with a trigger — stress, a life change, illness, a new baby. But it *continues* because of what happens next: you start trying to fix it.

You go to bed earlier to "catch up." You lie in at the weekend. You spend hours in bed hoping sleep will come. You start worrying about not sleeping, which makes it harder to sleep, which makes you worry more.

Your brain learns to associate bed with wakefulness, frustration, and anxiety — instead of sleep. That's how a few bad nights become months or years of insomnia.

The good news? If it's a learned pattern, it can be unlearned. That's exactly what SleepAssured does.

## How it actually works

### Your body has two sleep systems

Understanding these makes everything else click.

**Sleep pressure** builds up during the day, like a wave. Every hour you're awake, the pressure to sleep gets stronger. By the end of the day, that pressure is what pulls you into deep, restorative sleep. When you sleep, the pressure drains away, and the cycle starts again.

**Your body clock** is your internal 24-hour rhythm. It creates natural windows of sleepiness (late evening, early afternoon) and alertness (mid-morning, early evening). It runs best when you keep consistent sleep and wake times.

Good sleep happens when both systems line up — strong sleep pressure meets your body clock's sleepiness window. SleepAssured makes that happen.

### Sleep restriction: the engine of the programme

This is the part that sounds backwards but works brilliantly.

If you're lying in bed for 8 hours but only sleeping for 5, your sleep is spread thin — light, broken, and unsatisfying. Sleep restriction compresses your time in bed to match the sleep you're actually getting.

Yes, that means less time in bed at first. But here's what happens:

- **Sleep pressure builds higher** because you're awake longer
- You **fall asleep faster** — often within minutes
- You **wake less during the night**
- The sleep you get is **deeper and more restorative**

Once your sleep is solid and efficient, we gradually extend your time in bed. You earn back every minute — but this time, it's real sleep, not just lying there.

### Stimulus control: retraining your brain

Your brain is an association machine. If you've spent months lying in bed awake, frustrated, scrolling your phone — your brain now links bed with wakefulness.

We reverse that with a few simple rules:

- **Only go to bed when you're genuinely sleepy** — not just tired. Tiredness is low energy. Sleepiness is struggling to keep your eyes open. There's a difference.
- **Bed is for sleep only** (and intimacy). No phones, no TV, no lying there thinking.
- **If you can't sleep, get up.** Go to another room, do something calm in dim light, and come back when you feel sleepy again. This breaks the association between bed and frustration.
- **Same wake time every day.** Weekdays, weekends, holidays. This anchors your body clock.

It feels strict at first. But within weeks, your brain relearns that bed means sleep — and falling asleep becomes effortless again.

### Changing how you think about sleep

Insomnia rewires how you think. You start believing things that feel true but aren't:

> *"I need 8 hours or I can't function."*

Most people need between 6 and 9 hours. And your body is far more resilient to a short night than you think. The anxiety about not sleeping usually causes more problems than the lost sleep itself.

> *"If I don't sleep tonight, tomorrow will be a disaster."*

One bad night has surprisingly little impact on how you actually perform. What makes tomorrow hard is the dread and tension you carry about not sleeping.

> *"I've tried everything. Nothing works for me."*

Most things people try — supplements, apps, gadgets, herbal remedies — target symptoms. CBT-i is the only approach that addresses the root cause: the learned patterns that keep insomnia going. It works differently from anything you've tried before.

> *"I'll never sleep normally again."*

You will. Insomnia is not a permanent condition. It's a pattern your brain has learned, and SleepAssured is designed to unlearn it.

There's also something called the **sleep effort paradox**: the harder you try to sleep, the harder sleep becomes. Sleep isn't something you can force — it arrives when you stop chasing it. A big part of the programme is learning to let go of the effort.

## What to expect, week by week

### Baseline week
No changes yet. You simply track your sleep honestly. This gives us the data to build your personalised schedule.

### Weeks 1–2: The hard part
Let's be honest — the first two weeks are tough. Your sleep window is compressed, and you'll feel more tired during the day. That's normal, and it's temporary. The tiredness is the mechanism working — sleep pressure is building, and your body is learning to use its time in bed efficiently. This phase passes.

### Weeks 3–4: Things start clicking
You'll notice you're falling asleep faster and waking less. Your sleep is consolidating — becoming deeper and more solid. The daytime tiredness eases. This is where consistency pays off.

### Weeks 5 and beyond
Your sleep window gradually expands as your efficiency improves. You're sleeping more, sleeping better, and — most importantly — you're learning how your own sleep works. By this point, you're becoming your own sleep expert.
